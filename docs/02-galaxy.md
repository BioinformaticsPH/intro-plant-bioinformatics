# Module 2: Galaxy

The **Galaxy Workbench** is an open-source, web-based platform designed to make complex bioinformatics accessible to everyone, regardless of their "coding" or command-line experience. Traditionally, sequence analysis required high-level programming; Galaxy replaces this with a user-friendly, graphical interface.

* **The Workflow Philosophy**: In Galaxy, you don't just run one tool; you build Workflows. You can link together various steps—from raw data cleaning to final visualization—into a repeatable pipeline.

* **Reproducibility**: One of Galaxy's greatest strengths is its "History" system. Every analysis step is recorded, allowing researchers to share their exact methods and results with peers to ensure scientific transparency.

* **Toolbox**: It hosts thousands of integrated tools for transcriptomics (RNA-Seq), metagenomics, and proteomics, all accessible through a simple point-and-click system.
